Hello! My name is Maria Camarca, and I'm a postdoc at the National Radio Astronomy Observatory.

Our solar system retains an incredible treasury of planetary satellites, each with a unique geologic story to tell. From the colorful volcanic plains of Io to the heavily impact-scarred terrain of Callisto, there is a whole continuum of surface activity at the ready for us to learn from. I'm interested in using big telescopes on the ground and in space to study the thermal properties and composition of icy satellites.

My research in planetary science is currently supported by an NSF Astronomy and Astrophysics Postdoctoral Fellowship.
