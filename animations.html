Quality 3D Animations of Icy Satellites

Planetary astronomy can always benefit from excellent graphics and animations. I have collaborated with Thomas Halpin at AMGI Studios to produce some nice animations of icy satellites for use in scientific talks and outreach presentations. Jupiter's moon Callisto is arguably the Galilean satellite most in need of better marketing, so thus far we have focused on that moon. We also created an animation of the aurora on Ganymede base on results from our group's work. All animations are rendered in Unreal Engine using spacecraft image mosaics from the USGS Astrogeology Science Center. Important: Creative license has been taken in these animations. For example, texture was added to Callisto using albedo measurements, not actual topographic data.
